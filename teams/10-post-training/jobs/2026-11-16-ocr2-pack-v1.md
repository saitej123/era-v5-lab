## Resource basis (measured, 14 Nov build)

- Per shard, one worker: median 1,705 s, max 2,328 s on a 2-core Modal container. Four shards with `--workers 4` took
  1,249 s wall on an M1 Pro. **Estimate** for 70 shards on 8 cores with 6 workers: 12 rounds × ~1,250-1,700 s ≈ 4.2-5.7 h.
  The scaling assumption is that rounds do not slow each other; the pack stage's tokenizer threads compete with the other
  workers, which is not measured. The 360-minute limit is therefore tight. With 8 workers it would be ≈ 9 rounds, if memory allows.
- Memory: the largest single shard process peaked at 1,733 MB (Modal, children).
- Disk: the intermediates average 2.0 GB per shard (132.8 GB deleted across 66 Modal shards). The packs to keep are
  32.0 GB + 0.64 GB. Those working files alone are about 180 GB; this excludes the 30.397 GB of OCR-2
  parquet inputs pinned in the manifest, plus source snapshots and tool staging. Provision approximately 240 GB total
  free disk as an estimate with headroom, and measure the complete staged root before launching. The 240 GB envelope
  is not a measured full-run peak. Measured on the laptop's staged root (`du -shL`): sources 15 GB, cc 0.24 GB, Team 9
  tools + fingerprints 0.15 GB, so the inputs are ≈ 46 GB and the sum ≈ 226 GB before headroom.
